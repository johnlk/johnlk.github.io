The app is unsigned, so macOS blocks the first launch. The right-click Open shortcut no longer works. Apple removed that bypass in Sequoia, and Tahoe keeps it removed. For a trusted copy, use this one-time bypass:

  1. Double-click the app and click Done on the warning.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Click Open Anyway next to YouTube Music Wrapper, then confirm with your password or Touch ID.
